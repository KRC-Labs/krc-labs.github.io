Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Key operational manual and engineering questions for KRC Labs Showcase

🎨

Are theme selections and custom palettes preserved across page reloads?

Yes. All 6 deterministic themes and Custom Theme Studio palettes are persisted in local storage with zero flash of unstyled content (Zero FOUC) across F5 reloads.

🪟

How do I manipulate the floating guide and theme studio windows?

Drag the titlebar to reposition across the viewport. Use the bottom-right corner handle to resize, and click the minimize button or double-click the titlebar to dock as a slim minibar.

⌨️

What power keyboard shortcuts are supported?

Press '/' or Enter to focus real-time search, 'T' to cycle through all 6 themes, '?' to toggle the floating guide window, and 'Esc' to close active windows.
